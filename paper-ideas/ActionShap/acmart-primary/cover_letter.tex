\opening{Dear Editor-in-Chief,}

Please consider our manuscript, ``ActionShap: Evaluating Recommendation Explanations Beyond Deletion with Bounded Interventions,'' for publication as an Original Research article in \emph{ACM Transactions on Recommender Systems}.

We confirm that this work is original, has not been published previously, and is not currently under consideration by another journal or conference. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission to ACM TORS.

The manuscript addresses the evaluation of explanations for recommender systems. Its principal contributions are:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item ActionShap, an offline protocol distinguishing deletion faithfulness from alignment under bounded, executable profile interventions.
  \item A unified audit of pointwise alignment, signed validity, and budgeted decision quality under one temporal protocol and an exact budget-two oracle.
  \item User-level inference, matched null controls, convergence diagnostics, and explicit treatment of constant effect vectors and inactive oracles.
  \item A multi-dataset study with quality-gated ItemKNN and profile, sequential, graph, full-catalogue, and prospective robustness analyses.
\end{enumerate}

The work fits TORS's scope through its focus on new evaluation protocols and metrics for recommender systems, with particular emphasis on reproducibility and the validity of explanation evaluation. A reviewer-visible supplementary PDF contains the expanded robustness matrices, statistical validation, replication results, and computational-cost analysis.

\textbf{Artifact:} the deposit is assembled from the repository by \texttt{make artifact}, which archives the protocol and baseline code, preprocessing and configuration files, every experiment runner and its JSON output, the generated tables, the validation and integrity tests, the run notebook, and the machine-readable result matrices, with a per-member checksum manifest and a \texttt{.sha256} for the archive itself. Both documents are typeset against the content hash that same manifest freezes. The archive is supplied with this submission as reviewer-visible material, so no external link is needed to audit it during review; a permanent identifier will be minted at revision and quoted in the revised cover letter, because an identifier cannot exist before the deposit is made.

\textbf{Preprint status:} no preprint of this work exists, none is planned, and the manuscript is not under consideration at any other venue.

The authors used OpenAI Codex for LaTeX formatting, consistency checking, and language editing. The authors reviewed and edited all resulting material and take full responsibility for the manuscript. This assistance is disclosed in the Acknowledgments section.
