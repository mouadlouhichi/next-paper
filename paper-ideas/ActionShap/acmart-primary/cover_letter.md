Please consider our manuscript, “ActionShap: Evaluating Recommendation Explanations Beyond Deletion with Bounded Interventions,” for publication as an Original Research article in ACM Transactions on Recommender Systems.

We confirm that this work is original, has not been published previously, and is not currently under consideration by another journal or conference. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission to ACM TORS.

The manuscript addresses the evaluation of explanations for recommender systems. Its principal contributions are:

1. ActionShap, an offline evaluation protocol that distinguishes deletion faithfulness from alignment under bounded, system-executable profile interventions.
2. A unified audit of pointwise alignment, signed directional validity, and budgeted joint decision quality under one temporal protocol and an exact budget-two oracle.
3. User-level inference, matched null controls, convergence diagnostics, and explicit handling of constant effect vectors and inactive oracles.
4. A multi-dataset empirical study with ItemKNN as the quality-gated primary recommender and profile, sequential, graph, full-catalogue, and prospective analyses as robustness boundaries.

The work fits TORS’s scope through its focus on new evaluation protocols and metrics for recommender systems, with particular emphasis on reproducibility and the validity of explanation evaluation. A reviewer-visible supplementary PDF contains the expanded robustness matrices, statistical validation, replication results, and computational-cost analysis.
