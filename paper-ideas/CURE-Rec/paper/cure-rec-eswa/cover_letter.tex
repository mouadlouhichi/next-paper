\opening{Dear Editor,}

We submit the enclosed manuscript, ``CURE-Rec: Constrained Portfolio Selection for Sequential Recommendation via Fully Enumerated Cooperative Games'', for consideration as a research article in \textit{Expert Systems with Applications}.

Recommendation platforms deploy intervention portfolios (repetition caps, exploration and long-tail slots, diversity reranking, provider balancing), yet these transformations interact, compete for slate capacity, incur costs, and are sometimes required to repair a constraint-violating base policy. Isolated ablations cannot support such portfolio decisions. Our paper introduces a decision layer in which a bounded library of executable policy transformations are the players of a fully enumerated cooperative game: coalition values are produced by disclosed sequential rollouts, explained with exact Shapley values and pairwise interactions, and optimized directly under hard relevance, fatigue, provider-disparity, and budget constraints with explicit improvement/repair/abstention semantics.

The work fits the scope of ESWA because it is an expert-decision support methodology with a complete evaluation programme: nine controlled regimes verifying intended structures and misspecification; a 20-seed behavioural study; one-at-a-time and Latin-hypercube stress tests; sensitivity of the decision to the normative utility weights and the joint constraint frontier; predeclared divergent-configuration hold-outs in which the exact planner demonstrably outperforms singleton and marginal-gain heuristics; a learned-ranker base-policy integration; and an audited MovieLens-1M experiment with user-level paired inference (McNemar, bootstrap, permutation, Holm correction). Claim boundaries are enforced throughout: simulator-conditional results are never presented as real-world causal policy evidence.

All results are reproducible: the code, configurations, checksumed result snapshot, and a closure notebook for the remaining long-compute runs accompany the repository referenced in the manuscript.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors have approved the submission. The authors declare no competing interests.

Thank you for your consideration.
